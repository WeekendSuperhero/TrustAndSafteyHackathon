# SF Impact Lab Submission and Judging

← Back to [[SF Impact Lab Index]]

> [!important] Hard Deadline: 8:30 PM Sharp
> Submit early on the web hub! You can re-open and edit your web submission any time before 8:30 PM using any teammate's email. Judges always see your latest save.

---

## 3-Step Submission Checklist (Before 8:30 PM)

- [ ] **Step 1: Register Your Team (7:15 PM)**
  Go to [claudesocal.com/sf-impact-lab#team](https://claudesocal.com/sf-impact-lab#team), enter teammates' emails (up to 5 people; solo is fine), and write down your **Team Number** (`Team 1`, `Team 2`, etc.).
- [ ] **Step 2: Submit on the Web Hub (Before 8:30 PM)**
  Go to [claudesocal.com/sf-impact-lab#submit](https://claudesocal.com/sf-impact-lab#submit):
  - Project name + 1-sentence summary
  - Problem, who it's for, and what it does
  - Full link(s) a judge can open (GitHub repo, live demo URL, or both)
  - **60 to 90 second video link** (Loom, YouTube, or Google Drive)—*strongly recommended so judges shortlist you faster!*
  - Closest track tag
- [ ] **Step 3: Push `SUBMISSION.md` & Hand In Your Team Card (By 8:30 PM)**
  Push your code and completed `SUBMISSION.md` to GitHub (make sure **zero API keys or personal data** are committed) and hand your physical team card to a judge.

## How Judging Works (8:30 PM to 9:30 PM)

### Stage 1: Shortlist (8:30 PM to 8:45 PM)
Every judge reads the submissions and team cards. Each judge picks the projects they want to see live (a 60–90s video and a crisp 6-question card get you picked fastest). **3 to 10 finalists** are called up to demo.

### Stage 2: Finalist Demos (8:45 PM to 9:30 PM)
You get **3 minutes live** with zero extra prep time. Hit three beats:
1. **The Concern:** Specific person and real-world failure.
2. **Live Run on Real Data:** Show a real row/email/crash/policy on screen, your tool running on it, and your measured metric with sample size.
3. **One Honest Limitation:** What still slips through or what the data cannot tell you, plus who is accountable if it fails.

### Stage 3: Scoring Rubric

| Web Hub Finalist Rubric (Scored 1–5, Equal Weight) | Repo Card Rubric (Scored 1–3) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Impact (Tiebreaker):** Would people be safer or better off if this shipped next month? | **1. Real Concern:** Came from the room, with a specific *who* and a failure a neighbor recognizes. |
| **2. Works:** Live demo on real data. It actually runs. | **2. Works on Real Data:** Runs on real data and shows a measured finding with sample size. |
| **3. Scale:** A believable path to reaching many more people (reward the practical path, not the biggest claim). | **3. Honest:** Names a real limitation and states who is accountable if it fails. |
| **4. Innovation:** A sharp, practical way to see or solve the problem. | **4. Tiebreak:** Would someone outside this room actually use it? |
