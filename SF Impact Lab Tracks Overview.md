# SF Impact Lab Tracks Overview

← Back to [[SF Impact Lab Index]]

> [!info] How Track Selection Works
> At **6:40 PM**, walk to the Conversation Corner for the track you care about most. Talk with your circle and fill out one card together at [claudesocal.com/sf-impact-lab/cards](https://claudesocal.com/sf-impact-lab/cards).
> **Judging does not split by track**—all finalists compete in one pool.

---

## The Conversation Corner Formula (6:40 PM to 7:05 PM)

Every corner writes one shared card before splitting into build teams (up to 5 people per team):

> **How might we help `[who]` do `[what]` so that `[outcome]`?**
> **Build idea:** `A / B / C / D / E / our own`
> **We'll know it worked if:** `[what we'd see / measure tonight]`
